Recomposition core rule
Carb cycle around training
Run days: full carbs, full protein. Rest days: cut starchy carbs by ~half, keep protein identical at 85–90g. The surplus on run days builds muscle; the mild deficit on rest days burns fat. Scale stays similar — body composition shifts.
Protein — every single day
85–90g daily, non-negotiable
Spread across 4–5 meals. Sources rotate: fatty fish, chicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, tofu, tempeh, legumes, edamame. Never two identical protein sources in the same day.

Post-run window
Always eat within 30–45 min
Never skip recovery nutrition after hard sessions. Protein + carbs together. Skipping costs you the adaptation the session was meant to create.
non-negotiable on hard days
What to avoid
These slow recomp and accelerate aging
Alcohol, ultra-processed foods, excess added sugar, skipping breakfast on training days, under-eating on hard run days. You don't need to count calories — quality and timing do the work.
Green tea protocol
2–3 cups daily
Morning on empty stomach (boosts fat oxidation). Between meals, not with meals (can reduce iron absorption from plant sources). Avoid within 2h of bedtime.
